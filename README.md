# Morgan-stanley-power-BI-dashboard
# 📊 Morgan Stanley Banking Data & Risk Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze transaction volumes, portfolio account balances, and credit-risk metrics across multi-regional divisions. The objective is to provide executive leadership and compliance teams with real-time risk exposure monitoring and customer segmentation.

---

## 🔑 Key Insights & Analytics
* **Portfolio Scale:** Monitored **$44.16M** in overall transaction volume and **$58.21M** in total account balances across **180 unique clients**.
* **Risk Compliance:** Segmented high-risk accounts (Risk Score > 0.9) identifying **25 high-risk clients** requiring immediate compliance review.
* **Credit Score Breakdown:** Analyzed credit rating distributions (ranging 306 to 848, with an average of **568.53**) across customer tenure bands (Avg. 120 months).
* **Product Distribution:** Tracked account volume drivers across Savings Accounts (21.88%), Mutual Funds (20.13%), Credit Cards (19.88%), and Home Loans (19.5%).
